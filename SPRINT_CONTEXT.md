3. Order parts 2 to 3 weeks before they are needed. Parts are the bottleneck in India.

5. Fix one thing at a time. Write the guess first.
6. 2-hour rule: stuck for 2 hours means stop and ask in the project Discord with a clear question.
7. Daily log every day, 15 minutes, same day.
8. One X post every Sunday. One YouTube video per finished build.
9. Every week: message 2 builders with a specific question, leave 5 real replies.
